Using the demo

Create a Lab account to connect Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, or Notion. You may also try chat and local file analysis without connecting an account. Connect only accounts and content you are authorized to use, and select only the source items you want Lia to analyze.

There is no Bueeld Lab subscription or payment step in this demo. Shared AI usage, account capacity, and source reads are limited; access may pause or end as the hackathon demo changes.

AI output

Lia can make mistakes or miss context. Treat suggestions, source summaries, mission scores, and maturity indicators as working aids. Check important claims and make your own decisions before acting. The demo does not execute tasks in your external accounts.

External services and your data

Google and Notion connections are authorized through their own consent screens. AI analysis uses AdaL and the connected ChatGPT account. Their service terms and privacy practices also apply when you use them. See our Privacy & AI use page for what this demo stores and sends.

Availability

The demo is provided as available for hackathon testing. Its hosting, connections, AI access, and saved data can be interrupted or unavailable. You can export anything important from your own source accounts and delete your Lab account from the Account panel.
