Using the demo

Create a Lab account for saved project work and live Lia analysis. Visitors can answer a published interest test without an account; the fictional guided example is also available without live AI. Maturity indicates progress toward the first-pilot rubric in this demo; it does not guarantee commercial success. A public interest response is not a sale or proof of a unique customer. Check important claims and make your own decisions before acting. The demo does not execute tasks in your external accounts.
