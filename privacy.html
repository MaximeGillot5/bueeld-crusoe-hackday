Privacy & AI use

You choose what Lia sees. Connecting an account gives the demo read access, but Lia analyzes only the items you select.

Updated September 28, 2026

Your Lab account

We store your email, a salted password hash, saved chats, onboarding answers, project memory, selected source summaries, and mission progress on the private demo server. Signed-in chats save automatically. Guest drafts stay in your browser until you choose to save them to a Lab account. Account sessions use an HTTP-only cookie.

Connected sources

Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar request read-only access. Notion access is limited to the pages you approve in Notion. The demo shows a short list of available items; you choose which items to import and analyze. It does not send email, edit files or events, or change Notion pages.

OAuth access tokens, source lists, and raw imported content stay in temporary server memory and are not written to the Lab account store. Tokens expire or are removed when you disconnect, sign out, delete the account, or the server restarts. You can also revoke access in your Google or Notion account.

What goes to the AI

When you click Analyze, the selected source excerpts or extracted file text are sent to AdaL, which uses the connected ChatGPT account to generate Lia’s response. A chat request sends your current message, limited recent conversation context, project memory, any pinned mission, and summaries you selected for that chat. File contents are extracted in your browser; the original file is not saved by the demo server.

Provider content is not added to a saved chat by merely connecting. An AI reply or saved source summary can still contain details from a selected source, so review the items you choose before analyzing them.

Your controls and demo limits

You can disconnect a source, remove a selected summary, delete chats, or delete your Lab account in the Account panel. Account deletion removes its stored data and revokes its active Lab sessions. Guest browser drafts can be cleared in the browser. This is a temporary hackathon demo running through Vercel and Cloudflare to a private server; availability and long-term storage are not guaranteed.
