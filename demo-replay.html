The fictional example at a glance

  1. Context: a founder is testing the first booking experience for independent tutors.
  2. Mission: observe 3 tutors using a prototype. Target: at least 2 finish unaided within 2 minutes. The founder approves the test.
  3. Result: choose between 3 successful bookings or only 1 successful booking, with confusion at “Confirm slot”.
  4. Learning: a missed target changes the mission to clarifying confirmation and repeating the test. A met target shifts the next question to rescheduling.
