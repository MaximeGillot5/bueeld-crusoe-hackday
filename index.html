              Privacy & AI use

              Shared demo AI calls: checking allowance…

              Your chat and mission drafts are saved in this browser; signed-in work also syncs to this BUEELD server. When you ask Lia for analysis, the relevant message, saved project context, mission and selected source summaries are sent by the server to Crusoe Foundry. If Crusoe credits are exhausted, the same request is sent through AdaL using the connected ChatGPT account. Files are read in your browser when you choose Analyze; selected extracted text is then sent for analysis. Connected items are sent only after you select them and click Analyze. Your responses and evidence may be stored on this server so you can resume your project. Lia's reviews are recommendations; publishing a test and validating a mission remain your decisions.
